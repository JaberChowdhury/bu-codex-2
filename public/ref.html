Read before you compile

Who can participate?

Any current BU student, in teams of exactly three. Mixed-batch and mixed-department teams are welcome.

I don't have two teammates yet — can I still register?

Registration requires a full team of three. Post in the department's programming-club channel to find teammates before the window closes on Sep 05.

Is the onsite final mandatory?

Yes. The preliminary round is online and remote; teams that clear it must attend the final in person at the BU Computer Lab Complex.

What do I need to bring on finals day?

Student ID and a valid photo ID. Laptops are optional — contest machines are provided at each station.

Something changed after I registered — who do I contact?

Email the organizing committee before the event with your team name and the correction.
